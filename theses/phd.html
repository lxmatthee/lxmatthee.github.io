Isolated Micro-grids and Electromagnetic Compatibility: Analysis, Mitigation Techniques, and Measurements of Rapid Load Changes

Doctoral dissertation · University of Twente · 2024

Abstract / overview

This dissertation investigates electromagnetic compatibility and power quality challenges caused by rapid load changes in isolated and low-inertia microgrids. It compares generator- and inverter-based responses, examines measurements from laboratory and field environments, and investigates fast mitigation techniques using FPGA-controlled power electronics.
